About

From intern to operations lead

I sit between the people who set direction and the people who build, and I make sure the work between them runs on time, on record and with less risk.

I started at Divulge Gait Consulting as a social media marketing intern and grew into operations analyst, then operations manager, building the reporting and documentation the firm used to scale. I then sold fintech products to banks and microfinance institutions at Lidya and advised clients at Leadway, before returning to Divulge Gait as Business Operations Lead.

I am completing an MBA in General Management at the University of Lagos and working toward GRC credentials, with a focus on controls, risk assessment and data protection under the Nigeria Data Protection Act.
