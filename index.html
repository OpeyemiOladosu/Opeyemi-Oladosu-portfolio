About

From intern to operations lead

I began at Divulge Gait Consulting as a social media marketing intern and moved up to operations analyst and then operations manager. That is where I learned to run processes, write things down and report on them.

From there I moved into fintech sales at Lidya, selling to banks and microfinance institutions, then into financial advisory at Leadway. I am back at Divulge Gait as Business Operations Lead, working between stakeholders and technical teams. Alongside this I run several ventures through Prolific Jay Company, which I founded in February 2020.

I am completing an MBA at the University of Lagos and building a path into GRC: controls, risk assessment and data protection under the Nigeria Data Protection Act.
